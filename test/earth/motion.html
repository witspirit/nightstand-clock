Centered globe · 90% diameter · 28% opacity by default. Results and instructions · Still compositions

Motion applies only to pre-rendered views: discrete 30° steps, one circuit in at least six minutes, independent of clock time. This is accelerated illustrative rotation. Lighting is an artistic flat gradient selected by appearance time, never an astronomical sunlight calculation. Original shading remains baked in. No current clouds or weather.
